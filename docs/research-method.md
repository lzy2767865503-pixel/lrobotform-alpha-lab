# Research Method Notes

## Appropriate Interpretation

The simulator is a questionnaire-design aid. It helps researchers inspect how
item count, response dispersion, shared covariance, and sample size may affect
internal consistency.

An Alpha value between `0.70` and `0.90` can be a useful diagnostic range in
some contexts, but it is not a universal quality guarantee. Interpretation
depends on the construct, dimensionality, population, item wording, and
research purpose.

## Synthetic Versus Empirical Data

Synthetic rows are generated by an explicit mathematical process. They are not
observations of people and must not be included in empirical findings. A real
study still requires participant recruitment, consent, sampling justification,
data protection, and appropriate statistical analysis.

## Suggested Workflow

1. Review the construct and item wording.
2. Export the authorized questionnaire structure.
3. Run several documented seeds and sample sizes.
4. Inspect distributions rather than relying only on Alpha.
5. Revise ambiguous or redundant items.
6. Conduct a genuine pilot with consented participants.
7. Report synthetic and empirical stages separately.
