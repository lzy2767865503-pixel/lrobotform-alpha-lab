# Ethical Use

Lrobotform Alpha Lab is designed for questionnaire development, teaching,
reliability experiments, and authorized pressure testing.

## Required Conditions

Use the toolkit only when:

- you own the questionnaire or have written authorization from its owner;
- the generated rows remain clearly labelled as synthetic;
- the work does not impersonate human participants;
- the results are not represented as genuine survey evidence;
- real participant collection follows the applicable consent, privacy, ethics,
  and institutional requirements.

## Prohibited Uses

Do not use this project to:

- inflate response counts;
- fabricate a sample for coursework, publication, evaluation, or commercial
  claims;
- automate submissions to live forms;
- bypass anti-abuse systems or platform limits;
- collect personal data without proper authority and consent;
- claim that a target Alpha value proves validity or research quality.

## Reliability Interpretation

Cronbach's Alpha describes internal consistency under specific assumptions. It
does not establish construct validity, causal validity, representativeness,
absence of bias, or ethical acceptability. Synthetic calibration is a design
diagnostic and must remain separate from real empirical findings.
